The company

Built at the point where two unrelated crafts turned out to need the same discipline.

Neoux Industrial Solutions started from a simple observation: a website that breaks under real traffic and a seal that fails under real pressure fail for the same reason — someone specified it for the easy case, not the real one.

We work across two worlds that rarely share a company. On the digital side, freelance web design and development for businesses that need a site they can actually run their operations through. On the industrial side, sealing solutions, machine tool support, engineering consultancy and sourcing for plants that can't afford downtime.

The name comes from that overlap — a single fold that holds two different materials together without either one losing its shape. That's the mark in our logo, and it's the standard we hold both sides of the business to.

"Whether it's a line of code or a line of contact on a seal, the job is the same — hold under conditions worse than the average day."

— Founding note, Neoux Industrial Solutions

We're a young company, deliberately. That means direct access to the people doing the work, pricing without agency overhead, and a build list still short enough to give every client full attention.
